# Weather Terminal (web)

The web version of Weather Terminal: forecasts, air quality and a live map in
a terminal-style interface.

**Open it: https://emmalegrottaglie.github.io/weather-terminal/**

- **Now** — current conditions, today at a glance and the next hours.
- **Hourly** — the next 24 hours as a chart and a table.
- **Daily** — ten days with each day's range.
- **Atlas** — a live rain, temperature, wind, cloud and pressure map.
- **Air** — the European air quality index, pollutants and the UV index by hour.

It adapts to the browser window: one column on a phone, two columns from
600px wide, and a centred layout on wide monitors. Eight languages, four
colour palettes and three typefaces are in Settings.

## Privacy

No account, no tracking and no server of its own. Settings, saved places and
cached forecasts stay in your browser's local storage, and
**Settings → Delete all data** removes them. Forecasts come from
[Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) and the map from [Windy](https://www.windy.com/).
Privacy policy: https://emmalegrottaglie.github.io/weather-terminal-privacy/
